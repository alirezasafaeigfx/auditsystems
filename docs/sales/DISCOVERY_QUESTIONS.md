# Discovery Questions

## Initial qualification call

### Technical
1. What platform is your site built on? (WordPress, custom, Shopify, etc.)
2. Who currently manages your website?
3. How often do you update your site?
4. What's your current hosting setup?
5. Do you have any existing monitoring or analytics?

### Business
6. What's the primary goal of your website? (sales, leads, information, brand)
7. How many visitors do you get per month?
8. What's your current conversion rate?
9. What's the average order value?
10. What's your biggest website frustration right now?

### Pain points
11. Have you noticed any specific issues? (slow pages, errors, low traffic)
12. Have you lost visitors or sales recently?
13. Have you had any security concerns?
14. What's your budget for website improvements?
15. When would you want to start seeing improvements?

### Decision process
16. Who else is involved in the decision?
17. What's your timeline for making a decision?
18. Have you worked with other technical consultants before?
19. What would a successful outcome look like for you?
20. How do you prefer to receive updates? (email, phone, dashboard)
