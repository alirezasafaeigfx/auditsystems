# Lead Qualification Criteria

## Score lead by these signals:

### Hot (call within 24h)
- Email captured + free audit run + score < 60
- Email captured + visited /pricing
- Email captured + visited /unlock (tried to pay)
- Business email domain (@company.com not @gmail)
- Has ecommerce site (Shopify, WooCommerce, PersianSaz)

### Warm (nurture sequence)
- Email captured + free audit run
- Downloaded sample report
- Visited pricing page
- Blog subscriber

### Cold (long-term nurture)
- Email only, no audit run yet
- Newsletter subscriber only
- @gmail or @yahoo emails without audit activity

## Rejection criteria
- Competitor sites (other audit platforms)
- Adult content
- Gambling or illegal services
- Non-Persian market (our audit is Persian-optimized)
- Empty/no site (just domain squatting)
