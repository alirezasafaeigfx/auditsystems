# Audit Packages & Pricing

## Package 1 — Quick Scan (Free)
**Price:** 0 TOMAN
**Delivery:** Instant (automated)

- Single-page URL audit
- Lighthouse-based score (performance, SEO, accessibility)
- Top 5 findings summary
- No PDF export
- No manual review
- No delivery call

### When to offer:
- Every inbound visitor
- Lead magnet on homepage, blog, landing pages
- Collect email to deliver the free report → capture leads

---

## Package 2 — Professional Audit (Starter)
**Price:** 290,000 TOMAN
**Delivery:** 24-48 hours

- Full site audit (up to 3 pages)
- Performance, SEO, security, UX analysis
- Actionable recommendations with priority
- PDF report delivered
- Email support follow-up

### When to offer:
- After free scan shows medium/low scores
- Lead expresses specific concern (slow site, low conversion)
- Small business owners

---

## Package 3 — Enterprise Audit (Pro/Agency)
**Price:** 990,000 — 2,990,000 TOMAN
**Delivery:** 3-5 days

- Full site audit (up to 50 pages for Agency)
- Comprehensive technical analysis
- Security audit with OWASP checks
- Competitor benchmark
- Scheduled recurring audits
- PDF report + presentation
- 1-hour delivery call with findings walkthrough

### When to offer:
- Agency or ecommerce site
- Multiple concerns across categories
- Lead has budget authority
- Pre-scored low on free report
