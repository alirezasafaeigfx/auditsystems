This document distinguishes repository evidence from assumptions. It must be updated when authoritative product, customer, pricing, or market evidence changes.

## Product

AuditSystems is a web-based technical SEO and website audit platform. The repository describes automated website analysis, performance monitoring, technical SEO checks, content analysis, accessibility checks, background processing, and PDF reporting.

## Current Product Capabilities

Repository-backed capability areas:

- Technical SEO analysis
- Performance and Lighthouse-related checks
- Content-quality analysis
- Accessibility-related checks
- Competitor-analysis workflows
- PDF report generation
- Background jobs and scheduled audits
- Organization, report-access, and payment-related application flows
- Persian and English application surfaces

Capability presence does not prove completeness, accuracy, scale, or commercial readiness. Validate the relevant route and current behavior before making a specific claim.

## Repository-Stated Audiences

The README names these audiences:

- SEO professionals
- Website owners
- Agencies

These are current product hypotheses, not validated ICP priority. Customer interviews, revenue segmentation, win/loss analysis, and usage analytics are not available in this context.

## Positioning Guardrail

Use factual language such as “technical SEO and website audit platform” or “automated website analysis and reporting.” Avoid unverified superlatives such as “best,” “fastest,” “enterprise-grade,” or “complete.”

Do not claim the public service is live, healthy, secure, compliant, or production-ready without current authoritative evidence. Do not reuse timing or performance numbers from documentation unless a current reproducible measurement supports them.

## Core User Journey Hypothesis

1. A user submits or schedules a website audit.
2. The system processes checks in a background workflow.
3. The user reviews findings and remediation guidance.
4. The user may unlock, download, or share a report.
5. Teams may manage organizations, memberships, and recurring work.

Each step must be validated against current product behavior before it is used in campaign copy or analytics design.

## Messaging Principles

- Lead with clarity of findings and actionable remediation, not vague “AI” language.
- Explain scope and limitations of an audit.
- Keep trust, data handling, and report-access language precise.
- Write Persian copy natively for RTL readers; do not rely on literal translation.
- Use concrete outcomes only when measurement or customer evidence exists.
